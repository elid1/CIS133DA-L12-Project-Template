Our Commitment to Accessibility

We strive to create an inclusive online experience by making our website accessible and easy to use for everyone, regardless of their abilities or disabilities. We are continually working to improve accessibility and ensure all visitors can access our information and services.

Web Users and Web Accessibility

Disability Description Common Barriers Accessibility Principles to Apply
Auditory Hearing loss, difficulty hearing, or deafness. Videos without captions, audio without transcripts, and voice-only interactions. Perceivable: Provide captions, transcripts, and visual alerts.
Cognitive and Neurological Disabilities that affect learning, memory, attention, or processing information. Complicated instructions, confusing navigation, long text, and flashing animations. Understandable: Use simple language, consistent navigation, and clear instructions.
Physical Limited movement, muscle control, coordination, or dexterity. Small buttons, mouse-only navigation, and short time limits. Operable: Support keyboard navigation, larger buttons, and adjustable time limits.
Speech Difficulty producing speech that others or speech recognition software can understand. Voice-only commands and phone-only customer support. Operable: Provide text chat, email, and alternatives to voice commands.
Visual Low vision, blindness, or difficulty distinguishing colors. Poor color contrast, images without descriptions, and text that cannot be resized. Perceivable: Provide image descriptions, readable contrast, and resizable text.
